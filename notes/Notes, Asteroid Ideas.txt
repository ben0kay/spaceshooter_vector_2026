ASTEROID / WORLD GENERATION IDEAS

ASTEROID FIELD ARCHETYPES

- Lone Asteroids
  Very uncommon isolated asteroid groups. Usually 1, 2, 3 or 5 asteroids with lots of empty surrounding space.

- Small Dense Field
  Compact field with many closely packed asteroids. Strong navigation/combat obstacle.

- Large Sparse Field
  Broad regional formation with lots of open space, but should still contain local clusters so asteroid slowdown/navigation mechanics remain relevant.

- Mixed Scatter
  Loose irregular collection with no strong central density.

- Dense Core Field
  Sparse outer edge, becoming progressively denser toward the centre.

- Ring Field
  Hollow centre with asteroids distributed around a broad circular or elliptical perimeter.

- Crescent Field
  Curved partial-ring formation that creates a natural open flank.

- Broken Belt
  Long stretched asteroid band with gaps, breaks and uneven density.

- Twin Cluster
  Two major asteroid concentrations separated by open space.

- Archipelago Field
  Large overall region made from multiple dense asteroid islands with open space between them.

- Giant Anchor Field
  One to three HUGE asteroids acting as anchors, surrounded by groups of smaller rocks.

- Fragment Cloud
  Lots of small asteroids surrounding one or more larger broken bodies.

- Asteroid Chain
  Long string or snaking sequence of asteroid groups, potentially crossing a sector diagonally.

- Fractured Field
  Several medium sub-clusters with clear navigable lanes between them.

- Resource Vein
  A narrow path or band through a normal field containing a strongly biased ore composition.

- Rich Pocket Field
  Normal asteroid field containing one or more small high-value mineral concentrations.

- Mined-Out Field
  Mostly ordinary rock and debris, with very low valuable-resource density.


FIELD COMPOSITION / NESTING

Fields should support reusable subformations so generation can mix and match rather than hardcoding every possible field.

Example:

LARGE SPARSE FIELD
- loose background asteroid population
- 2-5 small local clusters
- optional SMALL DENSE pockets
- rare rich mineral pocket
- possible HUGE anchor asteroid

This keeps the field macro-sparse while still producing meaningful local terrain.

Possible optional subfeatures:
- dense pocket
- ore-rich pocket
- huge asteroid anchor
- rubble cloud
- hollow clearing
- narrow travel lane
- small ring
- rare lone valuable asteroid

Useful independent controls:

- ring
- crescent
- wedge
- arc
- diagonal lane
- hook
- S-curve
- multi-lobed organic blob


LARGE + SPARSE DESIGN NOTE

Large Sparse should not simply mean an enormous radius containing a low asteroid count. That can create gaps so huge that asteroid navigation and enemy slowdown mechanics barely matter.

Better identity:
- broad regional area
- lots of open travel space
- locally clustered asteroids
- several navigational choke areas
- optional dense pockets

Large Sparse should feel open on the macro scale but still contain interesting terrain on the local scale.

Avoid solving this only by adding hundreds more asteroids. Prefer deliberate clustering and smaller local formations.


ASTEROID MATERIAL / RESOURCE IDEAS

Current/general industrial-style resources can be mixed with rarer exotic deposits.

Normal resource ideas:
- Rock
- Iron Ore
- Copper Ore
- Carbon Ore
- Silicate Deposit
- Titanium Ore
- Sulfur Deposit
- Nickel Ore
- Cobalt Ore
- Tungsten Ore
- Lithium Deposit
- Ice
- Resonant Crystal Deposit

Possible exotic resources later:
- Phase Crystal
- Voidstone
- Plasma Quartz
- Xenolith
- Simulant Core Shard
- Alien Organic Chitin

Ordinary Rock should probably make up a large percentage of many natural fields so valuable ore-bearing asteroids stand out as deposits among common stone rather than every asteroid being valuable.


NON-ASTEROID WORLD GENERATION

- Dust Clouds
  Large soft visual regions that add atmosphere and could later reduce visibility or sensor range.

- Nebula Patches
  Coloured environmental zones with different visual identities and potential future gameplay effects.

- Ionized Zones
  Flickering electrical regions with arcs, particles and possible future shield/sensor interference.

- Gas Clouds
  Large drifting-looking clouds or pockets. Could eventually interact with weapons or engines.

- Micro-Debris Fields
  Tiny decorative fragments that create visual density without needing collision.

- Wreckage Fields
  Broken ship hulls, panels, pipes, engines and cargo debris distributed procedurally.

- Ice Expanse
  Region dominated by ice bodies and frozen fragments, with relatively few metals.

- Crystal Region
  Rare area with unusually high crystal concentration and distinct environmental visuals.

- Radiation Zone
  Mostly atmospheric initially, potentially affecting shields or equipment later.

- Magnetic Anomaly
  Debris arranged into strange arcs/rings or suspended-looking patterns.

- Gravity Anomaly
  Strange spatial feature that could eventually alter movement or projectile trajectories.

- Meteor Stream
  Narrow directional stream of small asteroid bodies crossing a sector.

- Comet Trail
  Long icy debris trail cutting across otherwise empty space.

- Empty Void Pocket
  Deliberately generated large empty areas inside otherwise active regions.

- Orbital Rubble
  Ring-like debris distribution suggesting an unseen or distant planetary body.


REGIONAL / MULTI-SECTOR GENERATION

Instead of every sector being generated completely independently, generate larger eastward regions spanning several sectors.

Possible region types:

- Void Zone
  3-5 or more sectors with almost no asteroids, resources or structures. Long-range scanner warns player before entry so they can fuel, repair and prepare.

- Rich Belt
  Several sectors with unusually high asteroid/resource density.

- Shattered Region
  Many small asteroid fields, rubble pockets and broken belts.

- Ice Expanse
  Several sectors biased heavily toward ice and volatile materials.

- Crystal Region
  Rare stretch with increased exotic/crystalline resources.

- Dead Industrial Corridor
  Mostly debris, exhausted rock fields and abandoned-looking space.

- Dust Sea
  Multiple sectors containing heavy ambient dust/nebula terrain.

- Fracture Zone
  Many isolated dense pockets separated by broad empty lanes.


LONG-RANGE SCANNER EVENTS

Regional generation can create scanner warnings before the player crosses east into unusual territory.

Examples:

LONG-RANGE SCAN
No significant asteroid density, industrial signatures or recoverable material detected ahead.
Estimated void span: 3-5 sectors.
Resupply before proceeding.

Other scanner messages:
- DENSE MASS SIGNATURES DETECTED
- INTERMITTENT ENERGY REFLECTIONS DETECTED
- HIGH MINERAL CONCENTRATION AHEAD
- NAVIGATION HAZARD: DENSE DEBRIS REGION
- NO RETURNS DETECTED BEYOND NAVIGATION LIMIT

Scanner upgrades could later improve the detail shown:
- estimated region length
- asteroid density
- dominant resources
- hazard level
- anomalous signatures
- unknown signals


LAYERED SECTOR GENERATION IDEA

Each sector can roll several independent generation layers instead of selecting one complete preset.

Example:

PRIMARY TERRAIN
Large Sparse Asteroid Field

SECONDARY TERRAIN
2 Dense Ore Pockets

AMBIENT
Light Dust Cloud

SPECIAL FEATURE
Broken Wreckage Belt

REGIONAL MODIFIER
Void-adjacent / mineral-rich / fractured / etc.

This creates lots of combinations without needing hundreds of authored sector presets.


SMALL EXTRA IDEAS

- Asteroid shadows or faint local dust halos around very dense groups.
- Rare gigantic inert rock that contains little resource but acts as a navigation landmark.
- Ore colour concentrated in cracks/veins rather than colouring the whole asteroid strongly.
- Fields whose density visibly increases in one direction.
- Rare perfectly clear corridor through a dense asteroid field.
- Tiny satellite-rock groups orbiting visually around HUGE asteroids.
- Resource pockets biased toward one ore but never guaranteed pure.
- Certain field shapes more common near specific regional types.
- Sector seed should make generated terrain reproducible for saves/debugging.
- Scanner map could eventually display rough asteroid-density blobs before the player physically reaches them.
- Large fields could have named/generated subzones internally for navigation and encounters later.
- Empty sectors should sometimes remain genuinely empty so dense/resource-rich regions feel more meaningful.
