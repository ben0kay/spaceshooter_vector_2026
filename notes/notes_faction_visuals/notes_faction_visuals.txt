FACTION VISUAL LANGUAGE

SIMULANT
Main hulls use void, hull_dark and hull_mid.
Never use pure black for the complete silhouette.
Primary vector outlines use purple.
Pink accent is reserved for small structural details.
Energy is used for weapons, conduits and moving components.
Core is the brightest colour and must be used sparingly.
Glow is reserved for low-alpha halos and emissions.
Most of the ship should remain dark, with neon identifying structure and active systems.

The exact RGB values are registered in faction_register.gml.
All Simulant primitive designs must use sc_faction_palette_get(Faction.SIMULANT).
