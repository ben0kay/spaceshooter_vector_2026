INTERNAL SHIP SYSTEMS - FUTURE FOUNDATION

Each player chassis registers:
engines, thrusters, shield_generator, reactor, weapons, sensors, cargo_hold, life_support.

Registered data stores condition_max. Player runtime receives an independent copy with condition_current initialized to maximum.

Currently dormant:
- No Step processing
- No system damage
- No stat penalties
- No repairs or UI
- No physical interior rooms

Future hull penetration may damage systems. System condition can then affect movement, shields, energy, weapons, sensors, cargo and survival.

Physical ship rooms should reference these existing system keys rather than creating a separate damage model.
