1. GENERIC ENTITY COLLISION FOUNDATION

Create an empty marker parent object:

o_entity

Make these children of o_entity:

* o_player
* o_enemy

The parent should not contain gameplay behaviour. It exists so projectiles and future area attacks can target all damageable entities through one collision category.

Give player and enemy a shared entity contract such as:

entity: {
    faction: Faction.PLAYER,
    damage_script: sc_player_damage
}

Enemies should use their registered faction and:

damage_script: sc_enemy_damage

Do not make consumers inspect whether the target is specifically a player or enemy. They should call the registered damage callback.

Use s_collision_circle as the initial shared collision mask.

Set mask scaling from each entity's collision radius. This is an acceptable internal use of image_xscale/image_yscale because it controls the invisible collision mask; visible drawing must continue using draw_angle, scale_x and scale_y.

Keep visual scale separate from collision-mask scale.


2. GENERIC PROJECTILE COLLISION

Move projectile impact detection out of the manual projectile Step distance check.

Add an o_projectile collision event with o_entity.

The collision handler should:

* Ignore the projectile owner
* Ignore the owner's faction
* Ignore friendly targets unless friendly fire is explicitly enabled
* Build or use the projectile's registered damage packet
* Call the target's registered damage_script
* Destroy or penetrate according to projectile behaviour
* Trigger impact visuals later
* Prevent one projectile damaging the same target repeatedly unless designed to do so

The generic collision code must not directly name:

* o_player
* o_enemy
* Simulant
* Shard
* Any specific weapon

Remove the old player-only manual projectile collision from sc_projectile_update after the collision event works.

Use GameMaker's collision broadphase through simple collision masks. Do not scan every entity from every projectile.

Fast projectiles may eventually require swept collision from previous position to current position to prevent tunnelling.


3. ATTACK DELIVERY AND AREA SHAPES

Keep damage delivery separate from damage calculation.

Add compact enums when the first non-projectile attack is implemented:

enum AttackDelivery { PROJECTILE, AREA, BEAM }
enum AttackAreaShape { CIRCLE, CAPSULE, CONE }

All delivery methods must eventually produce the same damage packet.

PROJECTILE:

* Moving instance
* Uses o_projectile → o_entity collision
* May later have health, penetration, ricochet or interception

AREA:

* One-time or repeating spatial query
* Circle for explosions and fields
* Capsule for beams and linear attacks
* Cone for directional attacks later

BEAM:

* Begins at a weapon hardpoint muzzle
* Extends toward an end point
* Uses a capsule-shaped damage area
* Visually updates every frame
* Damage may tick less frequently than drawing
* Must not apply damage every frame unless intentionally configured

A sustained beam should have:

* damage_interval
* next_damage_tick
* range
* width
* delivery
* area_shape
* damage_packet

Use a broad candidate query first, then an exact segment-to-circle distance test for capsule beams.

12. DAMAGE TYPES AND MATCHUPS

Current damage types should remain:

enum DamageType { KINETIC, ENERGY, EXPLOSIVE, ELECTRIC, THERMAL, CORROSIVE }

The central configuration should remain the single place for changing layer matchups.

Intended identities:

KINETIC
Strong against armour.
Weaker against shields.
Physical impact may later cause recoil or stagger.

ENERGY
Includes compact plasma pulses, energy bolts and laser-type energy damage.
Strong against shields.
Neutral or weaker against armour depending on configuration.

EXPLOSIVE
Good against hull and clustered targets.
May use circular area delivery.
Potentially less effective against intact shields.

ELECTRIC
Moderate direct damage.
Higher chance to apply equipment or module disruption.
May modify cooldowns, movement or power systems temporarily.

THERMAL
Weak against shields.
Set shield multiplier near 0.40 in central configuration.
Stronger once it reaches armour or hull.
May apply a heat or burning placeholder effect later.

CORROSIVE
Likely Alien faction identity.
Weak or moderate against shields.
Strong against armour and exposed hull.
May apply temporary armour-resistance reduction or damage over time later.

Do not store resistance matchups independently inside every enemy unless that enemy has a deliberate special resistance.

Global damage-type matchups belong in the central config.

Per-entity resistance modifiers, if later required, should be applied after the global matchup.
