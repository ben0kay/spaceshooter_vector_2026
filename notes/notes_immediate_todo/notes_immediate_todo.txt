4. PLAYER DAMAGE PIPELINE

Implement one proper player damage function with this routing order:

Shield
→ Armour
→ Hull

Shield:

* Absorbs damage first
* Regenerates over time
* Regeneration waits for the registered recharge delay after damage
* Shield visual alpha follows the current shield ratio
* Shield flashes brightly when hit
* Shield disappears at zero

Armour:

* Absorbs damage after shield
* Does not regenerate automatically
* Can eventually be repaired using mined resources
* Armour visual selects the correct baked 100%, 75%, 50% or 25% state
* Armour overlay disappears at zero

Hull:

* Takes damage after armour is depleted
* Cannot be repaired normally in the field
* Must eventually be repaired at the base
* Hull visual selects the correct baked damage state
* Zero hull begins the player destruction pipeline

Damage must correctly carry excess damage from one defensive layer into the next.
