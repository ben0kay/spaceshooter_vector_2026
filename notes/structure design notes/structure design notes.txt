Player structure visual idea: use the asteroid-style polygon approach as inspiration, but not random jaggedness. 
For player structures, use controlled geometric irregularity—clipped corners, stepped edges, small protrusions, 
recessed bites, asymmetric extensions, and authored polygon points—to make large station sections feel more 
intricate and less like obvious rectangles/circles. Keep the shapes deterministic and baked, so they always look 
identical and cost almost nothing at runtime.
