SYSTEM MODEL

EQUIPMENT
- Major physical ship assemblies.
- Installed through the EQUIPMENT tab into EquipmentSlot positions.
- Equipment may enable complete ship capabilities.
- Equipment grades are optional.
- A modifier is affected by grade only when it explicitly contains:
      grade_scaled: true
- Missing grade_scaled means that modifier never scales with grade.

SYSTEM MODULES
- Small fixed-quality tuning units crafted from components.
- Installed through the SYSTEMS tab.
- Modules never have item grades.
- Modules modify existing ship systems through the normal stat pipeline.
- Installed modules are reusable and are not destroyed when replaced.
