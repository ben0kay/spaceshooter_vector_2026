FACTION VISUAL LANGUAGE

SIMULANT

CORE IDENTITY
Simulant ships are constructed from layered near-black armour, charcoal mechanical panels, cold gunmetal edges and restrained violet energy systems.
Their silhouettes should appear sharp, deliberate, symmetrical and technologically precise.
They should look manufactured by intelligent machines rather than assembled for human comfort.

COLOUR DISTRIBUTION
Most of every Simulant ship must remain dark.
Purple should illuminate and decorate the ship; it should not form most of the hull.
Neutral greys establish physical structure while violet identifies active technology.

PALETTE USAGE
void:
Used for deep cavities, barrel openings, gaps between armour and recessed machinery.

hull_dark:
Used for the primary silhouette and largest armour surfaces.

hull_mid:
Used for secondary armour plates, cannon housings and raised structural sections.

hull_light:
Used for smaller raised panels, panel borders and readable mechanical separation.

metal:
Used sparingly for gun barrels, structural spines, sharp highlights and exposed machinery.

outline:
Used for neutral construction lines and subdued panel edges.
Do not use bright purple as the default outline of the complete ship.

accent:
Used for thin recessed violet strips, identification marks and small structural details.

energy:
Used for active conduits, weapons, rotating mechanisms and powered components.

core:
The brightest palette value.
Use only for reactor centres, muzzle apertures and extremely concentrated energy points.

glow:
Used at low alpha around active energy, engines, weapons and cores.
Glow should support a shape rather than replace its structure.

DESIGN RULES
Use layered armour rather than one simple filled silhouette.
Use at least three neutral hull values to establish depth.
Keep energy details thin and intentionally positioned.
Use symmetry for standard Simulant military ships.
Break symmetry only when the ship's function clearly requires it.
Cannons and mechanical attachments should have visible mounts, rails, segments or apertures.
Large empty black areas should contain subtle panel boundaries or raised armour.
Bright core colour must remain rare.
Bosses may contain more energy systems, but their hull must still remain predominantly dark.

REFERENCE CONSTRUCTION ORDER
1. Establish the void and hull_dark silhouette.
2. Add hull_mid armour panels.
3. Add hull_light separation and raised plating.
4. Add sparse metal machinery and structural edges.
5. Insert thin accent trenches.
6. Add active energy components.
7. Add a very small number of core highlights.
8. Add low-alpha glow only after the physical structure is readable.

The exact RGB values are registered in sc_faction_register.gml.
All Simulant primitive designs must use sc_faction_palette_get(Faction.SIMULANT).
The Twin Fighter is the initial reference implementation for this visual language.

FACTION PLANNING

SHARED FACTION STRUCTURE
Each faction can define:

* Identity and colour palette.
* Damage particles and destruction effects.
* Thruster colours and visual language.
* Alert, investigation and flee doctrine.
* Preferred movement and attack styles.
* Weapons, projectiles and ship designs.

SHIP CLASSIFICATION

* Role: Fighter, Cargo, Miner or Support.
* Class: Light, Standard, Heavy or Capital.
* Rank: Common, Elite, Miniboss or Boss.
* Classification describes the ship; callbacks control its behaviour.

SIMULANTS
Visuals:

* Dark violet hulls, purple energy and bright pale cores.
* Sleek geometric ships, rotating cores and calculated symmetry.
* Clean energy thrusters and dark purple damage smoke.

Behaviour:

* Calculated movement, orbiting, strafing and precise formations.
* Rarely flee or call for help.
* Strong shields, beams, pulse weapons and advanced rockets.

Current ships:

* Twin Fighter.
* Skirmisher.
* Dreadwing elite.
* Dreadnaught boss.

REBELS
Visuals:

* Beige, brown and worn industrial metal.
* Scrap armour, exposed machinery, repairs and mismatched panels.
* Orange energy, smoky engines and physical ammunition weapons.

Behaviour:

* Aggressive, erratic and less disciplined.
* Flybys, unpredictable strafing and scattered attack patterns.
* More likely to alert allies or flee when badly damaged.

Current ships:

* Rebel Gunship.

Future weapons:

* Miniguns, cannons, rockets, bombs and improvised weapons.

CORP SYNDICATE
Visuals:

* Clean manufactured armour and controlled corporate colours.
* Standardized ship frames, serial markings and uniform turrets.

Behaviour:

* Formal formations, coordinated alerts and disciplined retreats.
* Damaged ships may flee toward larger allied vessels.
* Reliable weapons with organized firing patterns.

ALIENS
Visuals:

* Organic hulls, unusual silhouettes and living energy structures.
* Goo, blood, spores or biological leakage instead of smoke.
* Ships may pulse, flex, regenerate or change shape.

Behaviour:

* Swarming, surrounding and instinctive group movement.
* Biological projectiles, acid, spores, tendrils and regeneration.

AUTOMATED
Visuals:

* Ancient machinery, faded lights and remnant technology.
* Neutral or abandoned installations, drones and defence platforms.

Behaviour:

* Follows old programmed rules rather than faction doctrine.
* May ignore ships, defend an area or attack anything nearby.
* Other factions may engage or ignore Automated units.

VISUAL PIPELINE

* Every ship provides body, core, hardpoint, thrust and death artwork.
* Static primitive artwork is baked into sprites at startup.
* Optional hull and armour layers allow progressive visible damage.
* Faction damage effects run automatically at low hull.
* Large ships scale thrusters, smoke and destruction using radius and visual mass.
* Shields match each ship's elliptical collision shape.
