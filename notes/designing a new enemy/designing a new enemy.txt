Use sc_sim_twin_fighter.gml as the complete enemy implementation example.

Reuse existing registered weapons where suitable. If creating a new weapon:
- Put weapon behaviour and numerical combat parameters in sc_simulant_weapons.gml.
- Put the projectile registration, baked primitive drawing and projectile-specific particles in its dedicated projectile file.
- Keep enemy-specific hardpoints, thrusters, visual components and attacks in the new enemy file.
- Do not place faction-specific behaviour inside generic enemy, weapon, projectile or particle infrastructure.
- Give complete vertically compact GML functions and preserve the current registration architecture.
