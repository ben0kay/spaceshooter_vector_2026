VECTOR 2026 - VISUAL ART STYLE GUIDE

CORE RENDERING STYLE

All generated game assets should visually belong beside the existing Player Shard,
Simulant Dreadwing and Rebel Gunship artwork.

The project style is highly detailed top-down hard-surface sci-fi game artwork with
crisp vector-like silhouettes, layered metallic construction and controlled coloured
lighting. It is not flat minimalist vector art.

UNIVERSAL ASSET RULES

- Strict top-down or near-top-down game-asset perspective.
- Ships and directional equipment should face toward the right.
- Strong readable silhouette at reduced in-game size.
- Crisp dark outer outline.
- Layered armour plates with clearly separated panel shapes.
- Dark recessed machinery visible between armour sections.
- Use vents, seams, bolts, pipes, coils, sockets and mechanical insets where suitable.
- Metallic shading with detailed painted/concept-art surface rendering.
- Use controlled highlights rather than broad bright glowing edges.
- Functional lights may glow, but should not overwhelm the material details.
- Avoid flat cartoon rendering.
- Avoid minimalist icon rendering.
- Avoid photorealistic backgrounds and cinematic scene lighting.
- Avoid excessive bloom, fog or lens effects.
- No text, labels, UI, grid or environment.
- Isolated asset with transparent background.
- Centre the complete asset and leave safe empty margins.
- Do not crop any part of the asset.
- Preserve clarity when scaled down for use in GameMaker.

FACTION MATERIAL LANGUAGE

PLAYER

- Clean, advanced and sleek construction.
- Dark gunmetal structure.
- Layered silver-grey armour.
- Cyan energy channels and functional lights.
- Sharp aerodynamic panel shapes.
- High-tech but physically constructed, not holographic.

SIMULANT

- Black, graphite and dark metallic armour.
- Angular, predatory or unusual silhouettes.
- Restrained purple energy channels.
- Dense layered plates and recessed alien-machine mechanisms.
- Purple glow should remain thin and controlled.
- Avoid bright purple outlines around the entire asset.

REBEL

- Scrappy but believable industrial construction.
- Worn beige or faded tan armour panels.
- Dark charcoal machinery and exposed framework.
- Orange heat, engine and weapon accents.
- Occasional yellow-black hazard markings.
- Visible rust, scratches, chipped paint, bolts, pipes and repaired sections.
- Should appear assembled from practical salvaged parts.
- Still retain a deliberate readable silhouette and professional asset quality.

ITEM AND COMPONENT ART

Crafting resources, components, equipment and system modules use the same rendering
language as the ships:

- Isolated top-down or slight top-down mechanical game asset.
- Strong dark outline and readable silhouette.
- Layered metallic casing and recessed mechanical details.
- Real material identity must remain obvious.
- Use faction colours only when the item belongs to a specific faction.
- Generic crafting components use neutral dark metal plus material-specific colouring.
- Keep glow limited to powered components.
- Do not turn components into flat UI icons.
- No inventory-slot background should be included in the image.

Examples:

COPPER COIL
A tightly wound copper electromagnetic coil inside a dark industrial mounting frame,
with visible terminals, layered metal brackets and subtle warm copper reflections.

BATTERY
A compact armoured energy cell with dark metallic casing, inset terminals, panel seams
and restrained cyan charge indicators.

COOLANT MODULE
A compact square ship-system module with an armoured dark-metal frame, inset cooling
coils, small vents, pale cyan coolant tubes and a restrained cold glow.

REFERENCE PRIORITY

When generating an asset, attach one or more existing project assets as visual
references. The reference images determine rendering quality, line treatment, material
detail and lighting. The written prompt determines the new object, faction and shape.

Do not copy the exact silhouette or internal arrangement of a reference asset.
Create a new object using the same visual rendering language.
