RESOURCE / CRAFTING EXPANSION IDEAS

Goal:
Build a richer IndustrialCraft / Galacticraft / ARK-like production chain where asteroid resources have clear identities and feed ship systems, ammunition, modules, drones and machinery.

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
RAW -> MATERIAL -> PART -> PRODUCT

Each resource should have a recognizable gameplay identity so the player learns what finding that asteroid means.

====================================================================
PROPOSED NORMAL / INDUSTRIAL ORE EXPANSION
====================================================================

NICKEL
- Early-mid game.
- Refines into Refined Nickel.
- Used in engine housings, high-temperature components and superalloys.
- Natural fit for thrusters, turbines and reactor structures.

LITHIUM
- Mid game.
- Refines into Lithium Compound.
- Main battery / energy-storage resource.
- Used for Battery Cells, Battery Packs, Capacitor Banks, drones and shield systems.

COBALT
- Mid game.
- Refines into Refined Cobalt.
- Main magnetic / high-temperature resource.
- Used for Magnetic Cores, Field Coils, motors, advanced reactors and shields.

TUNGSTEN
- Mid-late game.
- Refines into Refined Tungsten.
- Very dense / heat-resistant.
- Used in armour penetrators, heavy armour, heat shielding, gun barrels and high-impact kinetic weapons.

PLATINUM
- Late game.
- Refines into Refined Platinum.
- Used for precision contacts, catalysts, sensor electronics and high-end reactor systems.

IRIDIUM
- Very late / rare.
- Refines into Iridium Matrix.
- Used for exotic containment, advanced reactor internals and prototype technology.

Avoid adding too many extra periodic-table ores without a gameplay identity. The player should immediately understand why each asteroid is valuable.

====================================================================
ENDGAME / FANTASY ASTEROID ORES
====================================================================

These should be extremely rare, visually unmistakable and mechanically strange.
They should NOT simply be better versions of Titanium.
Each should unlock a unique endgame technology family.

VOIDGLASS
Visual:
- Near-black translucent asteroid.
- Violet / indigo light trapped inside fractures.
- Surface appears to swallow nearby light.
- Could be unusually difficult to detect on radar.

Refined form:
- Refined Voidglass
- Voidglass Lens
- Null-Frequency Matrix

Technology identity:
VOID / STEALTH / SHIELD MANIPULATION

Possible uses:
- Signature reduction modules
- Stealth systems
- Exotic shield emitters
- Shield bypass / suppression weapons
- Dark beam weapons
- Voidglass armour inserts
- Advanced sensor cloaking

Unique asteroid behaviour ideas:
- Reduced radar signature
- Very dark except for internal fractures
- Faint projectile-light absorption effect
- Low yield but extremely high value

====================================================================
OVERALL DIRECTION
====================================================================

The goal is not simply more ores.
The goal is a large interconnected industrial catalogue where nearly every ship system visibly consumes manufactured components.

Good content targets:
- Armour
- Shields
- Reactors
- Thrusters
- Targeting systems
- Utility systems
- Auxiliary systems
- Ammunition
- Rockets / missiles / mines
- Drones
- Mining equipment
- Scanners
- Cooling systems
- Fuel systems
- Power storage
- Beam / plasma components
- Mechanical assemblies
- Electronics
- Advanced alloys
- Exotic endgame technologies

NORMAL ORES should support believable industrial manufacturing.

FANTASY ORES should unlock mechanics the player cannot obtain through ordinary materials.

This should make asteroid discovery meaningful because the player knows what each resource unlocks, while still preserving the excitement of discovering a truly strange late-game asteroid and immediately thinking:

"What the hell is THAT?"
